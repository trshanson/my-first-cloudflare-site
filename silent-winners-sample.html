The Sample Bait Shop

Fictional Town, Iowa

41 years in business · 2nd generation

In their words

The fish do not check a feed, and our customers do not either. We just have to be open.

The story

This profile is made up. The Sample Bait Shop is not a real business, and Fictional Town is not a real place. In the sample, the family has sold bait and coffee for 41 years. They know the regulars by their trucks. When the power goes out, they still unlock the door and put the coffee on.

The lesson for providers

Be the place people can count on when the day starts. You do not need a bigger audience to do that.
